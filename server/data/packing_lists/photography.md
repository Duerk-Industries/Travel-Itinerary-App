## Camera Body & Lenses
- DSLR or Mirrorless camera body
- Spare camera body
- Wide-angle lens
- Prime lens (e.g., 35mm or 50mm)
- Telephoto lens
- Lens caps
- Body caps

## Accessories
- Camera bag or backpack
- Tripod or monopod
- Spare batteries
- Battery charger
- Memory cards (High speed)
- Memory card case
- External hard drive for backups
- Lens cleaning kit (Microfiber, blower, fluid)
- Filters (Polarizer, ND, UV)
- Remote shutter release

## Lighting & Power
- Speedlight or flash
- Portable LED light
- Spare flash batteries
- Power strip

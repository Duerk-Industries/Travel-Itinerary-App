## Diving Gear
- Dive mask
- Snorkel
- Dive fins and booties
- Wetsuit or rash guard
- Dive computer
- Regulator (if owned)
- BCD (if owned)
- Surface marker buoy (SMB)
- Save-a-dive kit

## Documents & Money
- Certification card
- Dive logbook
- Dive insurance details

## Travel Gear & Accessories
- Dry bag
- Underwater camera or housing
- Whistle or signaling device

## Health & Toiletries
- Defog solution or baby shampoo
- Reef-safe sunscreen

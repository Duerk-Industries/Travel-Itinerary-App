## Clothing & Footwear
- Lightweight linen or cotton clothing
- Sun hat (wide brim)
- Beach sandals
- Water shoes
- Rash guard

## Health & Toiletries
- Reef-safe sunscreen
- After-sun lotion
- Insect repellent
- Anti-itch cream for bug bites
- Rehydration salts
- Motion sickness medication

## Travel Gear & Accessories
- Beach towel
- Waterproof phone pouch
- Dry bag
- Snorkel mask
- Swim fins

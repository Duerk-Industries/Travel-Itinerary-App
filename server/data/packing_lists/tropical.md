## Beach Essentials
- Reef-safe sunscreen
- After-sun lotion
- Dry bag
- Snorkel mask
- Swim fins
- Beach towel
- Waterproof phone pouch

## Clothing & Accessories
- Sun hat (wide brim)
- Polarized sunglasses
- Beach sandals
- Water shoes
- Rash guard
- Lightweight linen or cotton clothing
- Insect repellent

## Health & Hydration
- Rehydration salts
- Motion sickness medication
- Anti-itch cream for bug bites

Right of Withdrawal

Last updated: July 17, 2026

If you are a consumer residing in the European Union (EU) or European Economic Area (EEA), you have the right to withdraw from a distance contract within 14 days without giving any reason.

1. The withdrawal period

The withdrawal period will expire after 14 days from the day of the conclusion of the contract (i.e., when you sign up for a paid subscription).

2. How to exercise the right

To exercise the right of withdrawal, inform Bryan Duerk, doing business as WanderBunnies, of your decision by an unequivocal statement sent before the withdrawal period expires. You may use the model form below, but it is not obligatory.

Email: support@wander-bunnies.com
Postal address: 4 Dickinson Circle, Shrewsbury, MA 01545, USA

You meet the deadline if you send your withdrawal notice before the 14-day period has expired. We will acknowledge an email withdrawal using a durable medium, such as email.

3. Effects of withdrawal

If you withdraw from this contract, we will reimburse all payments received from you without undue delay and no later than 14 days after we are informed of your decision. We will use the same payment method used for the original transaction unless you expressly agree otherwise, and you will not incur a fee for the reimbursement.

If you expressly requested that the Service begin during the withdrawal period, we may charge a proportionate amount for the Service supplied before you notified us of withdrawal, where applicable law permits. A withdrawal right for qualifying digital content or a fully performed service is lost only where all legally required prior express consent, request, and acknowledgment requirements have been satisfied.

These withdrawal rules do not limit separate statutory remedies for a digital service that is defective or does not conform to the contract.

Content Moderation Policy

Last Updated: July 17, 2026

WanderBunnies is committed to maintaining a safe and respectful environment for all users. This document describes our procedures for moderating content in shared trips and chat features, as required by the EU Digital Services Act (DSA).

1. Reporting Illegal Content

If you encounter content that you believe is illegal or violates our Terms of Service, please report it immediately by emailing support@duerk.org with the subject "Notice of Illegal Content."

2. Moderation Process

We use a combination of automated filters and manual human review to identify and remove content that violates our policies. This includes:

  • Illegal activities or solicitation.
  • Harassment or hate speech.
  • Explicit or adult content.
  • Spam or malicious links.

3. Sanctions

Depending on the severity of the violation, we may:

  • Issue a warning to the user.
  • Remove the specific piece of content.
  • Temporarily suspend the user's account.
  • Permanently terminate the user's account.

4. Right to Appeal

If we take action against your content or account, we will notify you with the reasons for our decision. You have the right to appeal our decision within 6 months by contacting us at support@duerk.org.

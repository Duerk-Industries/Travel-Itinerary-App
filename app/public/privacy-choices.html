Your privacy choices

WanderBunnies is operated by Tristan Duerk. You don't need to sign in to read this page, and visiting it does not turn on any analytics.

Optional collection

Two things are optional and off unless you turn them on, each separately:

  • Product analytics: which features you use and whether tasks succeed, linked to a random pseudonym for your account. Never your location, trip text, messages or imported content.
  • Detailed diagnostics: app crash and performance details sent to Sentry, linked to a random pseudonym.

Sign in and open Account → Privacy to turn either one on or off. Refusing or withdrawing never removes trip-planning features or changes your price. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, product analytics stays off in that browser.

Delete or download your data

  • Delete analytics data in Account → Privacy erases analytics and diagnostics linked to your account, without deleting the account. You can follow the request in the app.
  • Export account data in Account → Privacy downloads a machine-readable copy of your data, including your privacy choices.
  • To delete your whole account, see Delete your account.

We do not sell or share your data

We do not sell personal data or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we do not use advertising identifiers or track you across other companies' apps and websites. We honor Global Privacy Control as an opt-out signal.

Questions, requests you can't make in the app, or appeals: email support@wander-bunnies.com. See the Privacy Notice for details and response times.

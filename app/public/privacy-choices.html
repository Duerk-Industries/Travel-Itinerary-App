Your privacy choices

Sign in and open Account → Privacy to manage Product analytics and Detailed diagnostics separately. Both are optional. You can refuse or withdraw either choice without losing trip-planning features or paying a different price. Collection is off until the relevant service setting is enabled and you choose to allow it.

Product analytics measures feature use and trip-planning flows. Detailed diagnostics sends app crash and performance details to help diagnose problems. Necessary account, security, quota and billing records are explained in the Privacy Notice.

When a browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, product analytics stays off there. To request deletion of analytics data already associated with your account, email support@wander-bunnies.com. You may also request account deletion.

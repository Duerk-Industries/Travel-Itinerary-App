Delete your WanderBunnies account

In the app or website, sign in and open Account → Delete Account. You can also request deletion by emailing support@wander-bunnies.com from the address on your account. We may need to verify that you own the account before acting on an email request.

Deletion removes your account and associated personal trip data where possible. Shared trip content may remain for other group members, and records needed for legal, billing or security purposes may be retained for the applicable period. See the Privacy Notice for details. You may ask what was retained in your deletion request.

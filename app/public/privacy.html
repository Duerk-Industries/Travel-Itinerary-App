Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 17, 2026

WanderBunnies ("we," "us," or "our") operates the WanderBunnies travel itinerary application. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share your personal information when you use our web and mobile applications.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you register, we collect your email address, name, and profile information. We may also collect authentication data if you use third-party login services (like Google).

Trip Data: We collect information about the trips you plan, including destinations, dates, lodging details, activities, transportation, and expenses.

User Content: We process documents, emails, and images you upload for the purpose of "ingesting" travel details into your itinerary.

Usage Data: We collect technical information about how you interact with our service, including IP addresses, device identifiers, and browser types.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide and improve our services, including:

  • Creating and managing your travel itineraries.
  • Facilitating collaboration between group members you invite.
  • Generating AI-assisted travel recommendations.
  • Processing payments via Stripe for premium features.
  • Communicating with you about your account or service updates.

3. AI and Third-Party Services

Artificial Intelligence: We use third-party large language models (LLMs) like OpenAI to generate itinerary plans. While we do not send your full identity to these models, trip parameters (like destinations and dates) are processed to provide recommendations.

Location Services: We use Google Maps and Places APIs to provide location data and maps. Use of these features is subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

Affiliates: We may share anonymized activity descriptions with partners like GetYourGuide to provide relevant tour suggestions.

4. Data Sharing and Collaboration

WanderBunnies is a collaborative app. When you join a trip or invite others, your name and the trip details you contribute will be visible to other members of that trip group.

5. Data Retention and Security

We store your data securely using industry-standard encryption and access controls (Firebase/Google Cloud). You can delete your account and associated data at any time through the Account settings in the app.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, please contact us at support@duerk.org.

Cookie Notice

Last Updated: July 17, 2026

WanderBunnies uses cookies and similar technologies (like local storage) to provide, secure, and improve our Service. This notice explains what these technologies are and how we use them.

1. What are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies are small data files placed on your device. Local storage is a similar technology that allows a website or app to store information locally on your device. We use these to remember your session, preferences, and login status.

2. Why we use them

Category Purpose
Strictly Necessary Required for you to log in, navigate the app, and use security features. The app cannot function without these.
Functionality Used to remember your settings (like temperature units or light/dark mode) and choices you make.
Performance/Analytics Used to understand how users interact with the app, identifying errors and high-traffic areas to improve the service.

3. How to control them

Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies through their settings. However, blocking strictly necessary cookies will prevent you from logging into WanderBunnies.

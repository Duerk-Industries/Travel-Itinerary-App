Cookie Notice

Last Updated: October 9, 2026

This notice explains how Tristan Duerk, doing business as WanderBunnies, uses browser local storage, mobile application storage, cookies, and similar device technologies when you use the Service.

1. Technologies we use

Cookies are small data files stored by a browser. Browser local storage and mobile AsyncStorage also save information on your device, but are technically different from cookies. WanderBunnies currently relies primarily on local storage or AsyncStorage for session continuity, preferences, and pending app activity. Enabled security or diagnostics providers may use their own cookies or similar identifiers.

2. Storage inventory

Technology or key Purpose Typical duration
stp.session and stp.session.token Strictly necessary authentication, account context, and session continuity. Normally up to 12 hours, until logout, or until the stored session is invalidated.
stp.session.lastTripByEmail Remembers the last active trip for an account. Until removed by the user, account cleanup, or clearing application storage.
stp.session.asyncItinerary Preserves the status of pending or recently completed AI itinerary activity across refreshes. Until the activity is cleared, account cleanup, or clearing application storage.
Preference, follow-code, and pending-write storage Remembers display choices, followed trips, pending invitations, and offline or pending changes needed for requested functionality. Until the associated choice or activity is removed or application storage is cleared.
wanderbunnies:public-reaction:… in local storage After you react to a public blog, stores a random identifier for that blog and your selected reaction so the same browser can change or remove it. It is not used for advertising or across other sites. Until you clear website data or the blog's stored reaction is removed from this browser.
Firebase App Check and Google reCAPTCHA, if enabled Helps confirm that requests come from the genuine application and prevent abuse. Determined by Google and the active security configuration.
Your privacy choices Records whether you turned optional product analytics and detailed diagnostics on or off. Stored with your account on our servers, not on your device, so no cookie is needed to remember a refusal. While your account exists (see the Privacy Notice for evidence kept after deletion).
Product analytics (optional, off by default) Only if you opt in: feature-use events are held in app memory and sent in small batches. Nothing is written to cookies or device storage. Cleared when the app is closed, you sign out, or you withdraw consent.
Sentry detailed diagnostics (optional, off by default) Only if you opt in: crash and performance data from the app, linked to a random pseudonym. The Sentry SDK is not started on your device until you opt in. SDK data is cleared when you withdraw consent; data sent to Sentry expires within 30 days.

3. Legal basis and choices

We use storage that is strictly necessary to provide a feature you request, authenticate you, secure the Service, or preserve essential application state. Optional product analytics and detailed diagnostics start only after you opt in, and you can withdraw as easily as you agreed.

Manage preferences: sign in and open Account → Privacy, or see Your Privacy Choices. A browser Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal keeps product analytics off in that browser.

You can clear website data through your browser and mobile application storage through your device settings. Clearing necessary session storage will sign you out and may remove preferences or pending activity. Browser controls may also block provider cookies, although doing so may interfere with authentication or security features.

WanderBunnies does not currently use advertising cookies. We will update this notice and provide any required consent choices before introducing advertising or cross-site tracking technologies.

4. Contact and related notices

Questions may be sent to support@wander-bunnies.com. See the Privacy Notice for information about personal-data processing and your rights.
